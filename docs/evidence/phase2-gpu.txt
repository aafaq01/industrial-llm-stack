## Flags and suspicious results
- vllm-t4-1.5b-fp16 c1 (flagged in all 3 repeats: SM clock p50 615, 555, 555 MHz vs max 1305, 1200, 1245). The one
  real anomaly. At c1 the FP16 server ran at about half the SM clock of every other cell (mean 605 to 691 MHz vs
  1012 to 1402 elsewhere) at the same ~66 W, and its ITL p50 (19.2 ms) is no better than FP16 at c8 (18.3 ms).
  r1 was the session's first measured load (GPU 62 C rising to 81 C) and had the highest clocks (mean 691 MHz) and
  throughput (53.8 tok/s vs 50.4 and 50.6). The cause of the low clock is not recorded (no clock event reasons or
  memory clock during runs). Consequence for the AWQ vs FP16 c1 comparison (115.2 vs 51.6 tok/s, AWQ c1 SM clock p50
  ~1045 MHz): the clock difference is a real confound, but its size and direction are unknown. A large effect is
  unlikely: batch-1 decode is memory-bound (the bandwidth check below puts both at ~130 to 150 GB/s), FP16 at c8 runs
  18.3 ms per step at ~1070 MHz vs 19.2 ms at c1 at ~575 MHz, and weight size alone predicts up to about 2.8x (observed 2.2x)
  (~3.1 GB FP16 vs ~1.1 GB AWQ read per token). Fix for a future run: GPU sampler now records memory clock and clock
  event reasons, and the notebook captures nvidia-smi -q at the end (run.py and build_notebook.py changed, notebook
  not rebuilt).
- vllm-t4-1.5b-awq c32 (flagged: CV 0.11 on TTFT p50; r3 1.050 s vs 1.282 and 1.259). FP16 c32 shows the same
  pattern (r3 1.059 vs 1.209 and 1.246, CV 0.08, under the limit). Throughput and E2E p95 agree within 1%. At c32
  TTFT p50 depends on how 32 closed-loop workers line up with scheduler steps; small, not a measurement error.
- Not flagged but noisy: 7B AWQ c8 TTFT p95 2.887 ± 0.645 s (the flag rules check TTFT p50, not p95); its TTFT p50
  (0.961 to 0.980 s) and throughput agree within 2%. TP=2 r1 warmup TTFT mean 1.811 s vs 0.709 and 0.726 (first requests on a fresh
  server, warmup only).
- No warmup flags, no errors, no repeat disagreement, no temperature >= 85 C.
- Too-good check: decode rates are within T4 memory bandwidth (320 GB/s): 1.5B FP16 c1 ~50 tok/s x ~3 GB weights
  is ~150 GB/s; 1.5B AWQ c1 115 tok/s x ~1.1 GB is ~130 GB/s; TP=2 c32 1121 tok/s vs 716 on one GPU is 1.57x on two
  cards without P2P. Nothing exceeds a physical bound.
