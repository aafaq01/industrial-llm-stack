## Cache handling
llama.cpp keeps prompts in its prompt cache (cache_prompt defaults to on) and the server flags were not changed.
The llamacpp container was restarted (docker restart) before each concurrency level, so repeat 1 of every level
starts with an empty cache and repeats 2 and 3 run with the cache filled by the earlier repeats. This needed one
run.py call per concurrency level instead of one call with --concurrency 1 4 8; every other argument is the same.
The loadgen does not record cached tokens per request (it keeps usage.prompt_tokens and completion_tokens only, and
llama.cpp timings are not stored). The cache share per repeat is computed by report.py from the /metrics snapshots
the loadgen already stores around the measured window:
  share = d(llamacpp:prompt_tokens_cached_total) / (d(llamacpp:prompt_tokens_total) + d(llamacpp:prompt_tokens_cached_total))
Check that prompt_tokens_total counts only processed tokens: processed + cached = 15485 in every repeat, which equals
the sum of usage.prompt_tokens over the 64 measured requests (also checked on the smoke files: 80 + 1857 = 1937).
The same sum in all 9 runs, and exactly 8192 = 64 x 128 decoded tokens, also show no other completion traffic
reached llamacpp during any measured window (Prometheus scrapes of /metrics and Docker healthchecks of /health did run).
Repeat 1 is not 0% cached: every prompt shares the Qwen chat-template prefix, and the warmup requests (last 2, 4 or 8
prompts of the list) are measured again later in the same run, which is why c8 r1 (8 warmups) has the highest share.

## Flagged cells and explanations (round 0: cells averaged over r1 to r3; superseded by the regime split below)
c1 FLAGS: CV 0.20 out tok/s, CV 0.16 TTFT p50, CV 0.55 E2E p95, "warmup effect" r1 15.5 vs 22.3 tok/s.
  Cause: prompt cache, not warmup length. r1 had 10% cache hits and spent 175 s of its 530 s in prefill (13890
  prompt tokens processed); r2 and r3 had 96% and spent 11 to 12 s. Decode speed is the same in all three (ITL p50
  41.4, 40.6, 41.6 ms), so the gap is prefill. TTFT p95 10.8 s (r1) vs 0.31 and 0.32 s (r2, r3) is the long prompts being
  prefilled cold vs served from cache. TTFT p50 moves less (0.30 vs 0.23 s) because most prompts are short.
c4 FLAGS: CV 0.26 out tok/s, CV 0.43 TTFT p50, CV 0.54 E2E p95, "warmup effect" r1 16.7 vs 27.6 tok/s.
  Same cause: r1 16% cached, 177 s prefill; r2 and r3 96% cached, 11 s prefill. r2 and r3 agree closely
  (27.5 vs 27.8 tok/s, TTFT p50 9.46 s both). With --parallel 2, two of four requests wait in the queue, which is
  why TTFT p50 is about 9.5 s even with a warm cache.
c8 FLAGS: CV 0.10 (0.104) out tok/s, CV 0.17 E2E p95. No warmup flag, but this cell is suspicious in a different way:
  r3 (96% cached) was slower than r2 (96% cached): 18.6 vs 22.3 tok/s, ITL p50 92.4 vs 76.3 ms, decode slot-s
  842 vs 701 for the same 8192 tokens. Prefill does not explain it (17.6 vs 14.9 s). No other completion traffic
  hit llamacpp (token sums above). The start-of-window snapshot shows prometheus 6%, grafana 8%, postgres 8% for r3 (all under
  3% in r2), which is small next to llamacpp's ~400%. Cause not proven. ITL p50 per third of the measured window
  (round 1, below) is 99.6 / 93.3 / 82.4 ms in r3 vs 71.9 / 79.2 / 78.8 ms in r2: the slowdown is largest at the
  start of r3 and fades, which fits a burst of contention at the start of r3 (from outside Docker or the small
  container bump above; host CPU was not sampled during the runs) better than thermal throttling, which would
  build up over time. Thermal or power throttling of the 15 W laptop CPU is therefore the less likely explanation. Cold r1 (26% cached)
  landed at 18.9 tok/s, close to r3, because at c8 queueing dominates and prefill is a smaller share of wall time;
  so r1 vs r3 being equal is not evidence that the cache does not matter at c8.
All cells: 0 errors in 576 measured requests; token counts from server usage in every record.
Not a flag but worth knowing: aggregate out tok/s barely scales (warm repeats: c1 22.9 and 21.7, c4 27.5 and 27.8, c8 22.3 and 18.6 tok/s)
because llama.cpp runs 2 slots on 4 threads; extra concurrency only adds queueing (TTFT p50 0.23 s, 9.5 s, 36 to 41 s).
c8 warm is lower than c4 warm, but c8 is also the cell with the unexplained r3 slowdown.
