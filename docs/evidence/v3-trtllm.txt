## Reading and caveats
- One T4, one session, 3 repeats per cell; fixed 128-token decode with ignore_eos (not natural stopping).
- TRT-LLM is faster at c1 (68.9 vs 55.7 tok/s, 1.24x) but the vLLM c1 cell ran at SM clock p50 642 MHz against
  1040 MHz for TRT-LLM, both power-capped; part of the c1 gap may be clock. At c8 they are close (1.02x). At c32
  TRT-LLM is slower (0.84x) with TTFT p50 2.86 s vs 1.11 s; likely the unfused context attention (--context_fmha
  disable on SM75) making prefill of 32 queued prompts slower, not proven.
- GSM8K: TRT-LLM minus vLLM +0.012 strict-match (CI -0.012 to +0.036) and +0.000 flexible-extract: no difference
  measurable on 250 items. Both with temperature 0 and no repetition penalty (vLLM --generation-config vllm; TRT-LLM
  0.14 maps temperature 0 to greedy).
- TRT-LLM is served by its own patched OpenAI example server, not trtllm-serve (0.15+) and not Triton; vLLM by
  vllm serve. Server overheads differ and are part of the measurement.
- Engines are TRT-LLM 0.14 (last release listing Turing); newer TRT-LLM may differ.
